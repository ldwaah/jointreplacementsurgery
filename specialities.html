Orthopaedic Specialities

Comprehensive orthopaedic care covering a wide range of conditions and advanced treatment techniques

Ligament Injuries

Advanced treatment for ACL, PCL, and other ligament injuries with state-of-the-art surgical techniques and comprehensive rehabilitation programs.

Muscle Tears

Specialised care for muscle injuries and soft tissue damage with personalised treatment plans and advanced rehabilitation protocols.

JOINT INJECTIONS

Variety of joint injections available- local anaesthetic & steroid, hyaluronic acid, platelet-rich plasma (PRP) and Arthrosamid.

Knee Arthroscopy

Keyhole surgery for knee joint conditions with minimal scarring and faster recovery times using cutting-edge arthroscopic techniques.

Hip & Knee Replacements

Primary and revision joint replacement surgery with the latest implant technology and personalized care for optimal long-term outcomes.

Robotic-Assisted Surgery

Precision robotic-assisted hip & knee replacement with enhanced accuracy, improved outcomes, and reduced recovery times.

Minimally Invasive Surgery

Advanced techniques for faster recovery and less scarring with smaller incisions and reduced tissue damage for optimal patient outcomes.

Women's Health

Specialised orthopaedic care in women's health context with expertise in menopausal joint pathology and pelvic health orthopaedics.
