Why women come to me

Nothing is wrong with your life.
That is exactly the problem.

On paper it works. You are capable, respected, the person everyone calls first. And somewhere underneath it there is a quiet, persistent sense that you have outgrown the life you built — and no language for saying so out loud.

  • 01 You are the one who copes

    Everyone else got to fall apart. You got competent instead — and now nobody thinks to ask how you are.

  • 02 Something happened, and you moved on too fast

    A loss, a diagnosis, a betrayal, a birth. You processed it in the gaps between other people’s needs.

  • 03 The achievement stopped working

    You got the thing. The relief lasted a fortnight. You are already looking for the next thing to prove.

  • 04 You have done the reading

    You can name your patterns beautifully. Insight has become another way of staying in control.

The method

How the work actually goes

Whether we meet in therapy, coaching or a course, the shape is the same. Naming this openly is deliberate: you should know what you are walking into before you pay for it.

01

Land

We start with what is true today, not with your history. Most women arrive performing wellness out of habit. The first job is to stop.

02

Name

We find accurate words for what you have been carrying. Precision is not an intellectual exercise — the nervous system settles when a thing is finally named correctly.

03

Unhook

We trace the pattern to its origin and separate what was survival from what is still choice. This is the part that takes courage, and the part that changes things.

04

Build

We put something in place of the old strategy — boundaries, decisions, a different relationship to rest — and we practise until it holds without me.

The aim is not lifelong dependence on a therapist. It is that you leave with an internal version of this room — one you can go into on your own, for the rest of your life.
