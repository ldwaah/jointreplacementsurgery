The first step

Tell me a little,
and I will tell you honestly

This goes straight to me — not to an assistant, not to a funnel. You will hear back within two working days with either a time for a call or, if I am not the right person, the name of someone who is.

What happens next

  1. You send this. It arrives in my inbox, and nowhere else.
  2. I reply within two working days, personally.
  3. We speak for 20 minutes, free. You prepare nothing.
  4. You take as long as you like to decide. Nobody follows up twice.

If cost is the issue

Say so in the message. Payment plans exist for everything, and a small number of reduced-fee places are held open at all times. Asking has never once counted against anybody.

What you write here is confidential and stored securely. It is used only to reply to you, and never added to a mailing list without you asking.

Still deciding?

No. “Something is not right and I cannot name it” is one of the most common opening lines I get, and one of the most workable.

No. The consultation is a conversation, not a sales call, and I do not chase. If you go quiet, I assume you had good reasons.

Then you cry on the call. It happens often, it is not embarrassing, and it is usually the first sign we are in the right place.
