Who this is for

If any of this is
familiar, we should talk

I work most often with women who are functioning well by every external measure and quietly struggling by every internal one.

  • 01Anxiety inside a capable life

    The racing mind at 3am that nobody at work would ever guess at. Panic that arrives without an invitation.

  • 02Burnout that looks like competence

    Still delivering, still reliable, running on something that ran out a while ago.

  • 03Low mood and the flatness underneath

    Not dramatic. Just grey, and heavier each month, and increasingly hard to explain away.

  • 04Perfectionism with teeth

    Standards that were once an asset and have quietly become the thing that is hurting you.

  • 06Living well with what hurts

    Health conditions, chronic pain, or a diagnosis that changed the shape of the future.

What you get

What the fee actually buys

Not an hour of conversation. A structured course of treatment with a trained clinician, and everything around it.

  • A full assessment. Before we begin, a proper formulation of what is keeping this going — the thing most people have never been offered.
  • Weekly 50-minute sessions at a time that is reliably yours, not shuffled each week.
  • A written formulation you keep — your own patterns, mapped, in plain English.
  • Between-session practice designed for the life you actually have, not an imaginary one with more time in it.
  • Progress reviewed every six sessions, openly, using measures — so improvement is something we can see rather than hope for.
  • A planned ending, with a relapse-prevention plan. You leave with the skills, not with a dependency on me.

And behind all of it

Monthly clinical supervision on every case. Continuing professional development each year. Accreditation that is reassessed, not awarded once and forgotten. Insurance, a written contract, and a complaints route that does not run through me.

You are not paying for fifty minutes. You are paying for the years that made the fifty minutes worth having — and for the fact that you never have to wonder whether the person opposite you is qualified to hold what you brought.

Honest answers

If you can wait and NHS talking therapies fit what you need, use them — genuinely, and I will help you self-refer. What private work buys is time (weeks not months), continuity (the same therapist throughout), a session length that is not squeezed, and choosing the person yourself. For some people that is a luxury. For others it is the difference between getting help and not.
