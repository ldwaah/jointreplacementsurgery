What I am like

Before you sit down
opposite me

People rarely ask this and almost always want to know it. You are deciding whether you can be your actual self in front of me — so here is what the room is like.

  • 01I am warm, and I am direct

    I will not let you explain yourself perfectly and feel nothing. I will also never make you feel foolish for trying.

  • 02I do not do blank-faced silence

    You will know what I am thinking. If I have a hypothesis about what is going on, I will share it and you can tell me I am wrong.

  • 03There is room for faith and for doubt

    Whatever holds you — spiritual, cultural, ancestral — belongs in the room. I will not treat it as a symptom.

  • 04We will laugh

    Often, and usually at the least appropriate moment. Grief and humour have always lived on the same street.

What accreditation actually protects you from

In the UK, “therapist” is not a protected title. Anyone may use it. Accreditation is how you tell the difference: assessed training, supervised clinical hours, a code of conduct, and a complaints process that does not run through the person you are complaining about.

It also means I know the edges of my competence. If what you are carrying needs someone else — a different modality, a psychiatrist, a specialist service — I will say so and help you get there. That is part of the job, not a failure of it.

What I believe

Five things I will
not pretend otherwise about

  • 01Coping is a skill, not a personality

    You learned it somewhere, for good reason. It can be unlearned without losing the parts of it you still want.

  • 02Insight is not change

    Understanding your pattern perfectly and living inside it anyway is the most common place capable women get stuck.

  • 03We live our lives in between opposites

    Two true things can sit in the same week. Most suffering comes from being told to pick one.

  • 04Rest is not the reward for finishing

    There is no version of your list that ends. Rest has to be taken, never earned.

  • 05You are allowed to want more

    Even if your life already looks like enough. Especially then.
