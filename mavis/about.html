What I believe

Five things I will
not pretend otherwise about

  • 01Coping is a skill, not a personality

    You learned it somewhere, for good reason. It can be unlearned without losing the parts of it you still want.

  • 02Insight is not change

    Understanding your pattern perfectly and living inside it anyway is the most common place capable women get stuck.

  • 03The body keeps the score, and the diary

    What you have not felt does not disappear. It simply books itself in later, usually at an inconvenient time.

  • 04Rest is not the reward for finishing

    There is no version of your list that ends. The rest has to be taken, never earned.

  • 05You are allowed to want more

    Even if your life already looks like enough. Especially then.
