Trailblazing Orthopaedic Surgeon Ms. (Dr.) Samantha Tross featured on the Powerlist 2026

Ms. (Dr.) Samantha Tross, the UK's first Black female orthopaedic consultant surgeon, has once again been recognised as one of the most influential Black figures in Britain with her nomination on the prestigious Powerlist 2026.

Celebrating its 20th anniversary, the Powerlist honours exceptional achievers who have made a significant impact in their fields. Ms. (Dr.) Samantha Tross joined an inspiring group of leaders, innovators, and cultural icons at the exclusive London event on Friday 24 October 2025, marking another milestone in her distinguished career.
