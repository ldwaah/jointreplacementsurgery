INTERESTS:      

My clinical and academic interest are Hip and Knee Replacement Surgery and conditions of the hip and knee. I enjoy teaching and am actively involved in teaching medical students and trainee surgeons, both in the UK and Guyana. I am also the Programme Director for Core Surgical Training at Ealing Hospital (UK) and Educational Supervisor for Foundation doctors, and I serve as an examiner for medical final examinations for Imperial College. 


Other activities include being the Ex Clinical Lead in Orthopaedics for the Enhanced Recovery Programme Ealing, Transfusion Committee as well as fractured neck of femur Pathway. I am an ex Associate Editor for the Journal of Medical Case Reports.


My passion is mentoring and inspiring children, particularly female and those of ethnic minority backgrounds to consider a career in surgery. In this regard I am affiliated with many organisations. I’m also passionate about creating ethnic diversity and equity in Surgery and am a founder member and Trustee of the British Association of Black Surgeons. (BABS). 

My non-vocational interests include Fine Dining, Ballroom and Salsa dancing, Travelling and Public Speaking.
